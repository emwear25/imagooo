# Community designs that allow selling prints

Checked on 2026-10-07. Only licences that allow **commercial sale of physical prints** are listed: CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA, plus explicit commercial grants. Models that are non-commercial (NC), MakerWorld "Standard Digital File License", "MakerWorld Exclusive" or based on trademarks or characters (Disney, Pokémon, Star Wars, F1, car brands, Catan…) were excluded.

**Rules for every listed product**

1. Credit the designer on the product page: title, designer, link and licence. The storefront does this automatically through `product.design`.
2. Screenshot the licence on the day you download the file. Licences are irrevocable, but designers sometimes change the page later.
3. Never resell or re-host the files themselves. Sell only physical prints.
4. CC BY-SA: if you share modified files, they must stay CC BY-SA. Selling prints is fine.
5. Don't market items with third-party brand names.

How much is actually usable: in a sample of about 1,000 popular MakerWorld search results, only **29 (about 3%)** were CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. The rest were Standard Digital File License (806), MakerWorld Exclusive (72) or NC/ND variants (90).

## Cults3D / MyMiniFactory / Thangs (licence label read on each model page)

Platform rules:
- **Cults3D:** "CULTS PU" is private use and **not sellable**. "CULTS CU" / "CU-ND" and CC0 / BY / BY-SA are sellable.
- **MyMiniFactory:** paid files use the Standard Digital File Store License (personal use only). Free files marked "Credit – … – Commercial" may be sold with credit and a link.
- **Thangs:** selling prints needs a designer's paid commercial membership; no free CC-sellable models were found.
