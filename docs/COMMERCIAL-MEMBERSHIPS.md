# MakerWorld designers with a Commercial Membership

Checked on 2026-10-07 through MakerWorld's public pages and API. Tier **prices and terms are only visible when logged in**, so open each profile while logged in to see them.

## How a MakerWorld Commercial Membership works

Source: makerworld.com/en/commercial-license-membership-agreement

- You subscribe to **one designer**, who offers up to 3 tiers. The licence is a **direct contract with the designer**; MakerWorld grants nothing itself.
- **The designer sets the terms for each tier**: for example revenue caps, which models are included, or attribution rules. Read each tier.
- The licence is valid **only while you're subscribed**. It's billed monthly in USD and auto-renews; if you stop paying, you stop selling those prints.
- You may sell **physical prints**, but never the files.
- The designer can change tier terms; changes apply to existing subscribers from the next billing period.

## Suggested starter bundle

1. **@fifindr**: the biggest general catalogue, with toys, fidgets, bookends and play food.
2. **@cipriani3design** or **@BotanyChic**: premium decor (vases and planters).
3. **@pinkywings** or **@hollowmaker**: dragons and articulated animals, the best sellers at markets.
4. Optional: **@vanessamatos**, for personalised name keychains.

**Before paying, check in each tier:**
- Does it cover **all** models or only selected ones?
- Is there a **revenue or print cap**?
- Does selling through your **own webshop** and at markets count as allowed use?
- Is **attribution** required?

Screenshot the tier terms on the day you subscribe and record them in `docs/COMMUNITY-DESIGNS.md`.
